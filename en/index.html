WELCOME TO GERPHYON

A voxel Survival-Horror with Dungeon Crawler and platforming elements. Explore dystopian environments in first person inside a huge Mansion, full of Monsters and mysteries to uncover!

The mansion

What awaits you

01

Friend

The "FRIENDS" are the monsters of the game: each one is truly unique and has an attack all its own. Attacks come from every direction: they can change the environment, attack you through the screen, flood you with sounds or visual glitches, force you to move in a very specific way… and much more. There's no fighting here: you survive!

02

Atmosphere (THE ROOMS)

The game takes place inside the largest mansion ever built, an immense and mysterious place designed to house the FRIENDS. Every wing of the mansion is a world of its own: you might go from a simple kitchen to an entire city set within its walls, from an amusement park to an abandoned hospital…

03

Replayable WIP

The game is designed to be highly replayable. Besides a wide range of achievements (real challenges to complete), the world generation also changes with every run, offering ever-different situations, paths and encounters. You can also find unique collectibles and see them again in the museum!

The story

A door that closes behind you

You're a kid who one night hears voices coming from an abandoned mansion. Driven by curiosity, you sneak inside… but as soon as you cross the threshold, the door behind you locks. Panic sets in, and the only choice is to keep going and find a way out.

Along the way you'll find notes left by Maxwell, a detective assigned to Case 892: a series of people who vanished without a trace. And according to Maxwell, this mansion has something to do with it.

Standing in your way are the monsters: some hostile, others surprisingly friendly. And they're the ones who will tell you something very strange…

“This is our life.” What is really hiding inside the mansion?
